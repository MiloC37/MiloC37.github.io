How the estimate works

The requested unit count is multiplied by the median price per residential unit for validated historical sales in the selected borough and building-size group. The range uses the 25th and 75th percentiles.

Financing figures include principal and interest only, not taxes, insurance, repairs, utilities, reserves, closing costs, or renovations.
